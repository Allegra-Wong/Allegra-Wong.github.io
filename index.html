Cosplay Head

A cosplay head inspired by the animated series Jujutsu Kaisen. The structure is constructed from aluminum wire, cardboard, paper mâché, foam clay, paint, and custom 3D-printed components. An Arduino-based control system uses servo motors and a Hall-effect sensor to actuate a blinking eye mechanism.
