About Me

I am currently a 3rd year Mechanical Engineering student at The Cooper Union. I enjoy blending art with mechanical design, and I am passionate about projects that heavily involve various fabrication methods, such as 3D printing, machining, and sculpting. During this semester I hope to gain more experience in mechanical design and machining. As part of this, I have joined The Cooper Union's Toboggan Team, where I am leading the design of the vehicle's brake system. In January I will be going to Canada with the rest of the team to compete in the Great Northern Concrete Toboggan Race.
